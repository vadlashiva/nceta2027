Getting Here

Travel Information

Nearest Airport

Jindal Vijayanagar Airport (VDY), Toranagallu, about an hour by road from Ballari.

Hubballi Airport (HBX) and Kempegowda International Airport, Bengaluru (BLR) offer more flights; Ballari is reachable from both by road or rail.

Nearest Railway Station

Ballari Junction (BAY), connected to Bengaluru, Hubballi, Hyderabad and Guntakal.

Taxis and auto-rickshaws are available from the station to the campus on the Ballari–Siruguppa Road.

By Road

KSRTC and private buses connect Ballari with Bengaluru, Hospet, Hubballi and Hyderabad.

The campus is near Sindhigeri on the Ballari–Siruguppa Road.
