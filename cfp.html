Author Guidelines

  • Submit original work that has not been published or submitted elsewhere.
  • Full papers of 6 to 8 pages including references, in PDF.
  • Remove author names and affiliations from the review version (double-blind review).
  • Similarity index below 15% (excluding references).
  • At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present.

Formatting Instructions

  • Use the IEEE conference template (two-column, A4), available from the IEEE Author Center in Word and LaTeX.
  • Times New Roman, 10 pt body text; figures and tables numbered and cited in the text.
  • References in IEEE style, numbered in order of citation.
  • File name: NCETA2027_FirstAuthorSurname.pdf.

Review Process

  1. Initial check for scope, format and similarity.
  2. Double-blind review by at least two technical program committee members.
  3. Decision e-mailed by 28 December 2026, with reviewer comments.
  4. Revised camera-ready paper due 4 January 2027.

Publication Information

  • Accepted, registered and presented papers will be published in the conference proceedings by mid-February 2027.
  • The proceedings are planned with an ISBN; the publisher and indexing details will be announced on this page.
  • Selected extended papers may be recommended to partner journals.
  • Papers not presented at the conference will not be included.
