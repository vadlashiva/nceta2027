About the Conference

Where Emerging Technology Meets Application

The National Conference on Emerging Trends and Applications (NCETA-2027) aims to provide a platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, students, and innovators to present and discuss the latest developments in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Quantum Computing, IoT, Smart Systems, Cyber Security, and emerging technologies.

About Kishkinda University

Kishkinda University is a state private university established under Karnataka State Act No. 20 of 2023 with TEHRD Trust as the sponsoring body.

The university is committed to quality education, research, innovation, entrepreneurship, and outcome-based learning.

The TEHRD educational ecosystem serves more than 12,500 students across multiple institutions in Ballari.
